# Rider gear -- SOURCE

| file | what | made by | licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Helmet_open.blend` / `.glb` | jet (open-face) motorcycle helmet with a clear shield: SCT1, POB1, TRB1, and the 白バイ PMB1 (tinted white) | generated by `blender/tools/make_placeholder_helmets.py` (2026-10-16), our own | project's own |
| `Helmet_full.blend` / `.glb` | full-face helmet with a dark visor: SSB1 | the same | project's own |

**For an artist.** The `.blend` is the source; rework it freely, then re-export with
`blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python blender/tools/make_placeholder_helmets.py -- --export-only`
(the generator never overwrites an existing `.blend` without `--force`). Keep the contract the runtime reads:

- the object's **origin is the rider's head-bone pivot** (`head_2`, the top of the neck); Blender +Z up, the face looks along -Y;
- material **`shell`** is the paint the game tints per rider (white on a 白バイ); `visor` / `visor_dark` the shield, `trim` the rim and liner;
- Empty **`grab`**: where the right hand holds the helmet while putting it on and taking it off.

Which helmet a bike uses: `helmet` in `tools/build_vehicle_scenes.py`'s bike rows -> `VehicleConfig.riderHelmet`
("open" when not given). A new kind is a new `Helmet_<kind>.blend` + `.glb` here and that one key.
Size: a large real shell (0.28 x 0.32 m) for the anime-sized heads; long hair shows through (user, 2026-10-16: fine for now).
